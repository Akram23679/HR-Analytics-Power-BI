Hires vs leavers by year

HiresLeavers

Hiring stays flat at about 1,100 a year, while leavers climb from 7 in 2001 to about 170 a year after 2018. The data has no hires after 2020, and 2026 covers only January to September.

Line chart with 2 measures. Hire Year and Term Year are different columns, so first make a year table: Table tools → New table Years = GENERATESERIES(2000, 2026, 1) Then two new measures: Hires = COUNTROWS(FILTER(HR, HR[Hire Year] = SELECTEDVALUE(Years[Value]))) Leavers = COUNTROWS(FILTER(HR, HR[Term Year] = SELECTEDVALUE(Years[Value]))) X-axis: Years[Value] (Don't summarize, type Categorical) · Y-axis: Hires and Leavers
